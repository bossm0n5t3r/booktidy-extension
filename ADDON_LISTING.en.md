# BookTidy: A Cleaner Way to Browse Book Search Results

Stop letting unwanted book listings clutter your search results. BookTidy is a focused Firefox extension that helps you organize supported book search pages by collapsing results that match your own publisher, author, or title rules.

## Key Features

- 🔍 Rule-based filtering: Add rules for publishers, authors, or titles you want to collapse.
- 📚 Cleaner search results: Matching book listings are replaced with a compact BookTidy notice instead of taking up the full result area.
- 📝 Clear collapse reasons: Each collapsed item shows why it was matched, so you always know which rule was applied.
- 👀 Reveal when needed: Click the `Show` button to temporarily display the original book listing again.
- ⚡ Automatic page handling: BookTidy evaluates supported search result pages after refresh and also detects dynamically added results.
- 🎨 Light and dark themes: Choose the popup theme that fits your browser environment.
- 🔘 Simple on/off control: Enable or disable BookTidy directly from the extension popup.

## Privacy

Your privacy matters. BookTidy stores your rules and settings only in Firefox's local extension storage. Your data is not sent to any external server, and BookTidy does not use analytics, advertising tracking, or remote data collection.

## Current Support

BookTidy currently supports Firefox Desktop and Kyobo Book Centre search result pages.

Install BookTidy and make your book search results easier to scan, review, and manage.
